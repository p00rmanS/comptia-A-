# Course audit — September 12, 2026

## Fixed

- Port recall compared formatting rather than complete port sets. It now accepts equivalent lists, orderings, and inclusive ranges while rejecting missing/extra ports and malformed answers. For example, `137,138,139` matches NetBIOS; `137/139` does not.
- Notes overwrote the storage-failure warning with a successful-save label. The save operation now reports success/failure, and the inline note status accurately reports a failed write.
- Dashboard and progress counts included obsolete saved lesson IDs while the percentage excluded them. All completion totals now count distinct current lesson IDs. Stored history is retained.

## Added

Four original guided lessons: IPv6 (2.6), display technologies (3.1), mobile-device troubleshooting (5.4), and Wi-Fi diagnosis (5.5 / 2.2). Each has English and Taglish explanations, analogy, five teaching steps, vocabulary, a worked paper lab, recap, related lessons, and three original checks.

The course contains 38 lessons, 23 guided workshops, and 114 checks. Existing lesson IDs, numbers, and answer ordering remain unchanged; new lessons are appended.

## Content sources and boundaries

Mappings were checked against the [official Core 1 V15 objectives](https://assets.ctfassets.net/82ripq7fjls2/1oSdlyujpaX3GrM0rir6Ge/91afb2be72785281e8fb4c0d9a70c6f4/CompTIA-A-220-1201-Exam-Objectives-3.0.pdf). Each mapping covers selected concepts, not necessarily the whole objective.

IPv6 details were checked against [RFC 4291](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc4291/), [RFC 4193](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4193.html), [RFC 3849](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc3849/), and [RFC 4861](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc4861/).

Display comparisons were cross-checked with [LG panel guidance](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-monitor-understanding-your-monitor-panel-CT10000030-20153229189600) and [Corsair panel explanations](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/explorer/gamer/monitors/monitor-panel-types-explained-va-tn-ips-oled-qd-oled/). Device-specific service procedures remain dependent on the manufacturer.

Liquid-warning handling follows [Apple's connector guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102643). Wireless diagnosis was cross-checked with [Cisco's wireless troubleshooting guide](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/smb/wireless/CB-Wireless-Mesh/1902-tz-Troubleshooting-Traditional-Cisco-Wireless-Network.html).

This audit found no remaining failures in the checks above. It is not a guarantee of zero defects across every browser/device, nor a full independent fact check of every existing paragraph. Full objective coverage, a timed exam simulator, and interactive performance-based labs remain unfinished. New labs are paper exercises with revealable solutions.
