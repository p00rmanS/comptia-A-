## Added teaching

Four original guided lessons append to the existing course without changing earlier lesson numbers or question ordering:

- PoE (2.5): sources, injectors, powered devices, standards, per-port capacity, total budgets, and a worked allocation example.
- Wireless standards (2.2): generation labels, band compatibility, channel width, link rates, and controlled troubleshooting comparisons.

Total: 47 guided lessons and 141 original questions. Each addition includes goals, English and Taglish explanations, vocabulary, four teaching steps, a worked paper lab, recap, related lessons, and three explained questions.

## Study improvements

Shuffle previously selected a random starting index and then continued in curriculum order. It now shuffles a copy of the full deck, maintains that order during navigation, and shows every card once before wrapping. Domain filters retain the shuffled relative order. Curriculum order and saved question identities remain unchanged.

Practice sessions now offer 5, 10, or 20 unique questions. A small domain or missed-question pool automatically caps the actual session length. The default remains five. Session length is shown through the existing question counter and completion summary.
