# Core 2 expansion — October 4, 2026

Added 12 guided English/Taglish lessons and 36 original checks, bringing Core 2 to 28 lessons and 84 checks. Each includes vocabulary, guided teaching, a worked paper lab, and explanations for all choices.

Topics: Windows editions (1.3), Windows settings (1.6), client networking (1.7), macOS (1.8), cloud productivity (1.10/1.11), wireless security (2.3/2.10), browser security (2.11), encryption (2.2/2.7), browser diagnosis (3.4), remote support (4.7/4.9), evidence/privacy (4.6), and scripting (4.8).

The three new content modules append after the existing 16 lessons. Compatibility digests protect the original four and previous 16 lesson question identities and rotated choices. Course storage keys remain separate and unchanged. Learning sequences cover every lesson once; new content joins search, flashcards, practice, review, notes, bookmarks, and completion tracking.

Coverage remains selected concepts. Labs are paper exercises; the app does not execute scripts or system commands. Evidence handling follows organizational authorization and policy rather than prescribing jurisdiction-specific legal requirements.
