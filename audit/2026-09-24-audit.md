# Teaching expansion — September 24, 2026

## New lessons

Added 18 original guided workshops, bringing the course to 65 lessons and 195 questions:

| Area | Lessons |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile | SIM/eSIM, location services, camera and microphone input |
| Networking | Subnet calculation, NAT/PAT, email DNS authentication, network appliances, time/logs/AAA |
| Hardware | Video connections, RAID capacity, laser print stages, inkjet/thermal/impact mechanisms |
| Virtualization and cloud | VM network boundaries, synchronization versus recovery |
| Troubleshooting | RAM/POST isolation, cable faults, measured performance, display artifacts |

Every addition has three learning goals, English and Taglish explanations, vocabulary, four teaching steps, a worked paper lab, common confusions, three recap points, related lessons, and three explained knowledge checks. The new scenarios encourage evidence-based reasoning and include realistic diagnostic alternatives alongside introductory checks.

The subnet lab works through /26 boundaries and explicitly limits ordinary-host counting assumptions. The RAID lab states equal-size-member and overhead assumptions and distinguishes RAID 10 failure placement. NAT/PAT is labeled as supporting routing context rather than claiming a new formal objective. Model-specific hardware procedures remain conditional on the manufacturer's documentation.
