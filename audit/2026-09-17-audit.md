# Course refinement — September 17, 2026

## Curriculum

- Expanded all 15 previously concise foundation lessons into guided workshops with three goals, vocabulary, at least four teaching steps, a worked paper lab, common confusions, a recap, and related lessons.
- Added containers/VDI (4.1) and managed mobile devices (1.3), with six original questions and explanations.
- Total: 43 lessons, all guided, and 129 questions. Existing lesson IDs and question ordering remain stable; the two additions are appended at positions 42 and 43.
- Preserved English, Taglish, technician explanations, and existing scoring behavior. Participation completion remains distinct from accuracy.
- Replaced the accumulated, contradictory README counts with current setup, feature, coverage, and verification documentation.

## App repairs and refinements

- Search now includes objective mappings, concept-flow text, primary scenarios, explanations, and additional lesson check content.
- Port search links open the matching exercise instead of the general tools page.
- Lessons resume at the first unanswered check in the current attempt. Past attempts and scenario practice do not incorrectly count as current-attempt participation.
- Direct check navigation permits reviewing any of the three questions. The completion control remains disabled until all three are answered.
- Failed note writes remain visibly unsaved after a section or theme rerender. Clearing a note reports when the deletion only affects the in-memory session.
- Replacing a lesson/flashcard surface resets unfinished gesture state.
- Added the missing favicon after browser logging identified a 404 request to /favicon.ico.
- Browser assertions now derive course totals from the lesson data rather than assuming 38 lessons. Error logs include the resource URL.
